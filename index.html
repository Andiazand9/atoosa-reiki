What is Reiki

An old practice, a simple idea

Reiki is a gentle Japanese healing practice built on one idea: that life energy moves through all of us, and that it can become blocked by stress, illness, or old tension. Through light touch or hands held just above the body a Reiki practitioner helps that energy flow more freely again.

A session is quiet. You stay clothed, lying down or seated, while the practitioner works through a series of positions. Most people describe it as deeply relaxing: a warmth, a lightness, sometimes sleep.

  • Eases stress and anxiety
  • Supports the body's own healing response
  • Helps release stuck emotional tension
  • Leaves you clearer, calmer, more grounded
